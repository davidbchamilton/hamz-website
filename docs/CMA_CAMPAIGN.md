# CMA Campaign

## Phase 1 - Before the Awards

- CMA homepage messaging active
- `/cma` prepared
- exclusive packs uploaded
- invite codes generated
- QR cards prepared
- analytics ready

## Phase 2 - During the Awards

QR cards lead to:

`https://producername.com/cma`

Artists:

1. scan QR code
2. enter email
3. enter invite code or use an invite already embedded in the QR URL
4. obtain access
5. preview/download packs

Track:

- CMA landing-page visits
- successful invitations
- pack views
- downloads
- marketing opt-ins

Unique QR links should eventually be supported:

`/cma?invite=ABC123`

Do not implement this functionality yet.

## Phase 3 - After the Awards

- remove or reduce time-sensitive CMA homepage prominence
- retain valid private access where appropriate
- publish legitimate event photography or results
- follow up only with contacts who have provided appropriate consent
- continue offering newer packs where appropriate
